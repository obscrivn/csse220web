## Purpose

AI assistants are becoming part of professional software development. In industry, AI-assisted work may need to be reviewed, explained, or audited.

This log gives you practice using AI in a way that is:

- transparent,
- intentional,
- explainable,
- focused on learning rather than outsourcing your work.

You are **not graded on how much AI you use**. Using no AI is completely fine.

You should not paste entire AI conversations into this file. Instead, briefly document interactions that were useful for your learning or debugging.
