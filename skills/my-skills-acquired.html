My Skills Acquired

A short record of each new skill you learned during the final project, with evidence.

Every time you learn a technique your team needed, write one entry. A good entry shows where the skill lives in your code, how you know it works, and what you can now do on your own. It is also the best preparation for explaining your work at check-ins and the final demo.

How this fits the course: this record is for you and your team. It does not add or change any milestone requirement or grading policy; follow your milestone pages for those. No certificates, badges, or outside accounts are needed. Skills you learned outside this hub count too (from a lab, documentation, a teammate, or an AI coach) as long as you give the same kind of evidence.

Completed example

An illustrative entry for a drone-game team. Your entries can be shorter, but they should be just as specific.

Skill: Score that is awarded exactly once (Skill 04)

Why the team needed it
Our Milestone 2 workflow is "collect an orb, then use the points to unlock the gate". The gate check needs a reliable score, and our first version gave hundreds of points for one orb.
What I learned
Score is model state, so I created a ScoreKeeper class with addPoints, getScore, and reset, and no JavaFX imports. checkCollision runs on every Timeline tick, every arrow key, and every Rotate, so the award needs a guard. A hidden Circle still has bounds, so crystal.isVisible() is what makes the award happen once. The view refreshes the label from the model with an updateScoreLabel() helper, the same pattern as Lab 12.
Where it appears in the code
src/main/java/demo/ScoreKeeper.java; ArrowApp.checkCollision(...) (award inside the visible-orb block); ArrowApp.updateScoreLabel(); the Reset handler in ArrowApp.start(...). Pull request #7, "Award orb points once".
How I tested it
  • Normal: Start with no steering → Score 10 (expected 10). ✔
  • Edge: after collecting, pressed arrows and Rotate 10 times → still 10 (before the fix: 390). ✔
  • Repeat: collect → Reset → collect, three times → 10 each time, as our design says. ✔
  • Check class: ScoreKeeperCheck, 4 checks including "reset clears score" and "negative points ignored". All print PASS.
Did AI help? How?
Yes. I used the Skill 08 debugging prompt with ChatGPT and pasted my checkCollision plus the reproduction steps. It asked how often checkCollision runs, which led me to add a print statement and find the repeated awards. I rejected its first suggestion (a second Timeline just for scoring) because our design keeps one Timeline. The guard condition is my own fix. Logged in AI-LEARNING-LOG.md, Week 8.
What I can now explain or do independently
I can explain why a guard condition prevents duplicate rewards and point to it in our code. I can add a new scoring event (finishing a level) the same way without help, and write a check for ScoreKeeper first.
